Academic CV of Alberto Morato — Research Fellow at the University of Padova, formerly at CNR-IEIIT. Focused on real-time communication networks, TSN, and Industrial IoT.

Summary My research is centered on real-time communication networks, with a particular focus on hybrid Wired-Wireless-5G Time-Sensitive Networking (TSN) systems. I specialize in the application of these networks in time-critical distributed measurement systems and the development of measurement techniques to assess their performance.
